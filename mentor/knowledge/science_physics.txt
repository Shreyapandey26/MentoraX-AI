SUBJECT: Physics
CATEGORY: Subject-Specific Study Strategies

Physics is a conceptual subject where formula memorisation without understanding leads to failure.
Understanding the "why" behind every formula is critical for scoring above 75%.

CORE WEAK AREAS IN PHYSICS:
- Mechanics: Newton's laws, motion equations, and energy conservation are most commonly tested.
  Strategy: Draw free body diagrams for every problem before writing equations.
- Electricity: Ohm's law, Kirchhoff's laws, and circuit analysis require systematic approach.
  Strategy: Redraw complex circuits step by step. Practice series vs parallel reduction.
- Optics: Ray diagram drawing is a high-scoring area that students often skip.
  Strategy: Practice drawing ray diagrams for all 6 mirror and lens cases daily.
- Thermodynamics: Laws and applications need conceptual clarity first.
  Strategy: Create a one-page summary of all thermodynamic laws with one real-world example each.
- Modern Physics: Photoelectric effect, nuclear physics require formula linkage.
  Strategy: Create a formula card grouped by topic — not alphabetically.

STUDY TECHNIQUE FOR PHYSICS:
1. Derivation Practice: Derive every major formula at least twice from scratch.
2. Numerical First: Always attempt numericals before theory MCQs — builds application skill.
3. Unit Analysis: Check units in every formula substitution to avoid silly errors.
4. Diagram Habit: For every concept, draw the associated diagram (ray diagram, circuit, graph).
5. Concept Verification: For every formula, verify it with 2 real-world examples.

FOR LOW SCORERS IN PHYSICS (below 50%):
- Focus on chapters with highest weightage: Mechanics, Electricity, and Optics (combined ~55% marks).
- Solve only NCERT exemplar problems — they match exam pattern closely.
- Find a study partner to explain concepts to each other.

FOR AVERAGE SCORERS IN PHYSICS (50-74%):
- Stop skipping derivations in exam — they carry guaranteed marks.
- Solve at least 1 numerical from each chapter daily.
- Attempt past 5-year question papers chapter by chapter.

FOR EXCELLENT SCORERS IN PHYSICS (75%+):
- Prepare for JEE-level numericals to build speed for standard exams.
- Focus on integrated problems that combine 2 or more chapters.
- Review all HOTS (Higher Order Thinking Skills) questions.
