CATEGORY: Alert Tier — Emergency Recovery Plan (Score below 50%)

IMMEDIATE PRIORITY ACTIONS (Week 1):
An Alert-tier student needs a structured emergency recovery plan, not generic advice.
The first step is accepting the current situation without shame and building a realistic plan.

STEP 1 — DIAGNOSIS (Day 1-2):
- List every chapter in the subject with a self-rating: Understood / Partial / No idea.
- Identify the 3 chapters with highest exam weightage — these become your primary focus.
- Talk to your teacher or subject faculty this week — get a clear list of must-do topics.

STEP 2 — REBUILD FOUNDATION (Week 1-2):
- Do not start with difficult problems. Begin with the very basics of weak topics.
- Use NCERT textbook only — no reference books until you finish NCERT fully.
- Spend 3 hours daily on the subject: 1 hour reading, 2 hours practice.
- Complete all NCERT examples and exercises in priority chapters.

STEP 3 — PRACTICE ESCALATION (Week 3-4):
- Attempt 10 questions daily from previous year exams — easy and medium difficulty only.
- Review every wrong answer in detail. Write the correct approach in an error notebook.
- Form a study group with 2-3 peers who are performing better — peer learning is highly effective.

STEP 4 — ATTENDANCE RECOVERY (if attendance is below 75%):
- Attend every class from today — no exceptions.
- Take notes during every lecture even if you do not understand fully.
- Meet the teacher after class for 10 minutes to clarify doubts from that lecture.
- Attendance below 75% means you are missing approximately 1 in every 4 classes.

STRICT DAILY SCHEDULE FOR ALERT TIER:
06:00 - 07:30: Primary subject study (most important topic)
07:30 - 08:30: Morning routine + breakfast
School / College hours
17:00 - 18:00: Review today's lecture notes
18:00 - 19:30: Previous year question practice
20:00 - 21:30: Secondary subject study
22:00: Sleep (non-negotiable)

MINDSET GUIDANCE:
- A student who scores 40% today can reach 70% in 6 weeks with consistent effort.
- Progress in the first 2 weeks will feel slow — this is normal. Do not quit.
- Track completion, not hours. Completed 20 NCERT problems = concrete progress.
- Avoid comparing with peers — focus on beating your own previous score.

COMMON MISTAKES ALERT-TIER STUDENTS MAKE:
- Studying for 8 hours one day, then not studying for 3 days — inconsistency is the biggest enemy.
- Starting with difficult topics — always build from the foundation up.
- Avoiding seeking help — teachers want to help struggling students, but students must ask.
- Checking phone during study — use app blockers during study sessions.
