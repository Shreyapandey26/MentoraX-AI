SUBJECT: Mathematics
CATEGORY: Subject-Specific Study Strategies

Mathematics requires consistent daily practice rather than bulk studying before exams.
The most effective method for mathematics is solving problems, not just reading theory.

CORE WEAK AREAS IN MATHEMATICS:
- Algebra: Students struggle with simultaneous equations, quadratic equations, and factorisation.
  Strategy: Practice 5 equations per day. Start with simpler forms before complex expressions.
- Calculus: Differentiation and integration concepts need visual understanding first.
  Strategy: Watch 10-minute concept videos before attempting problems. Draw graphs manually.
- Trigonometry: Memorising all formulas at once causes confusion.
  Strategy: Use a formula sheet and derive each identity once by hand to understand the logic.
- Geometry: Proof-based questions require step-by-step logical thinking.
  Strategy: Read solved proofs aloud and then attempt the same proof from memory next day.
- Statistics: Students confuse mean, median, mode in applied problems.
  Strategy: Create a comparison table and solve 3 problems of each type daily.

STUDY TECHNIQUE FOR MATHEMATICS:
1. Active Recall: Attempt every problem before looking at the solution.
2. Spaced Repetition: Revisit a topic after 1 day, 3 days, and 7 days.
3. Error Journal: Keep a dedicated notebook of mistakes. Review before every exam.
4. Timed Practice: Set a 2-minute timer per mark. Build exam-speed habit.
5. Concept Maps: Draw relationships between algebra, functions, and calculus visually.

FOR LOW SCORERS IN MATHEMATICS (below 50%):
- Start with Class 9 basics even if in higher grade. Weak foundations cause cascading failure.
- Solve 1 previous year paper per week under timed conditions.
- Seek help for problems you cannot solve within 10 minutes — do not waste time stuck.

FOR AVERAGE SCORERS IN MATHEMATICS (50-74%):
- Identify which topics you score highest in and target them for full marks.
- Practice higher-order application problems from reference books.
- Attempt 2 full mock papers per week.

FOR EXCELLENT SCORERS IN MATHEMATICS (75%+):
- Attempt Olympiad-level problems for deeper insight.
- Teach concepts to peers — the Feynman technique improves your own clarity.
- Focus on speed and accuracy for competitive exam preparation.

RECOMMENDED DAILY ROUTINE FOR MATHEMATICS:
Day 1: Revise 1 chapter theory (30 min) + solve 10 NCERT problems
Day 2: Solve 1 previous year paper section
Day 3: Error review from previous days + 5 new application problems
Day 4: Timed full chapter test
Day 5: Weak area targeted practice

Mathematics performance improves by an average of 15-20% with consistent 3-week practice.
